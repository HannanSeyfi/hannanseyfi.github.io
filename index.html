01 Current work

Learning to forget.

Research details ↗

Unlearning in large language models

My MSc thesis examines how to remove targeted facts from an adapted language model while preserving the knowledge it should retain. I use a controlled synthetic benchmark to measure both sides of this trade-off.

  • Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct
  • LoRA / PEFT
  • PyTorch
Explore research code ↗

A controlled evaluation

500
Synthetic facts
100 forget · 400 retain
1,500
Evaluation questions
Including held-out paraphrases

Gradient ascent reduced held-out forget accuracy from 92.5% to 34.0%, while retain accuracy fell from 91.9% to 66.9%—a measured forgetting–utility trade-off.
