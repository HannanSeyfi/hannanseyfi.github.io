01 / MSc thesis

Unlearning in LLMs

Unlearning in Large Language Models

April 2025–Present

Khatam University · Supervisors: Dr. Ehsan Akhtarkavan and Dr. Babak Majidi

My thesis studies the forgetting–utility trade-off in an adapted language model. A controlled benchmark makes it possible to distinguish targeted forgetting from broader loss of retained knowledge, including on questions phrased differently from the training data.

100Facts to forget
400Facts to retain
1,500Evaluation questions

Approach

  1. Constructed 500 synthetic facts and evaluation questions that include held-out paraphrases.
  2. Trained LoRA adapters for Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct using PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, and PEFT.
  3. Evaluated gradient ascent, retain/KL regularization, PCGrad-style projection, and adaptive and rollback constraints.
  4. Built reproducible GPU training and evaluation workflows, with versioned data, checkpoints, and experiment reports.
