Research experience

Unlearning in Large Language Models

Apr 2025–Present

MSc thesis research · Khatam University · Research code ↗

  • Constructed a controlled benchmark of 500 synthetic facts, with 100 forget and 400 retain facts, and 1,500 evaluation questions including held-out paraphrases.
  • Trained LoRA adapters for Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct and evaluated gradient ascent, retain/KL regularization, PCGrad-style projection, and adaptive and rollback constraints.
  • Characterized the forgetting–utility trade-off: gradient ascent reduced held-out forget accuracy from 92.5% to 34.0%, while retain accuracy fell from 91.9% to 66.9%.
  • Developed reproducible GPU training and evaluation workflows using PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, and PEFT, with versioned data, checkpoints, and experiment reports.
